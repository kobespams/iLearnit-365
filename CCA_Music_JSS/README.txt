1. OVERVIEW & CURRICULUM STANDARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This repository provides the complete, structured curriculum for Cultural and 
Creative Arts (CCA) - Music for Junior Secondary Schools (JSS 1 - 3), aligned 
with the National Educational Research and Development Council (NERDC) syllabus 
and WAEC/NECO/BECE assessment standards.

Key Learning Outcomes:
- Develop foundational music literacy: staff notation, solfa notation, rhythm, and pitch.
- Appreciate organology: African traditional instruments vs. Western orchestral families.
- Understand Western music history (Baroque, Classical, Romantic) and contemporary Nigerian genres.
- Master harmony, cadences, polyrhythms, African call-and-response structures, and SATB choir balance.
- Gain competence in music technology, audio production, copyright ethics, and career pathways.
